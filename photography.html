PHOTOGRAPHY CONSENT

Protecting children's privacy and safety

At Kidis Robotics, we enjoy sharing the creativity, learning and achievements of our students. We also respect every child's privacy and safety.

1. Taking Photographs

Photographs or videos may be taken during classes, workshops and special activities to capture learning experiences and student projects.

2. Using Photographs

Photographs may be considered for use on the Kidis Robotics website, social media pages or promotional materials, but only with the appropriate permission.

3. Parent or Guardian Permission

Permission must be obtained from a parent or legal guardian before a child's photograph or video is published. Families should be told where the images may appear.

4. Respecting Privacy

Families can ask questions about how photographs are used. Children's personal information should not be shared alongside photographs without appropriate permission.

5. Questions or Concerns

For questions about photographs or permission, please contact Kidis Robotics.
